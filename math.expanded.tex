\section{Algebra}

The quadratic formula:
\begin{equation}
    x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
\end{equation}

\section{Calculus}

The fundamental theorem of calculus:
\begin{equation}
    \int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a)
\end{equation}

\section{Linear Algebra}

Matrix multiplication:
\begin{equation}
    \begin{pmatrix}
        a & b \\
        c & d
    \end{pmatrix}
    \begin{pmatrix}
        x \\
        y
    \end{pmatrix}
    =
    \begin{pmatrix}
        ax + by \\
        cx + dy
    \end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}
